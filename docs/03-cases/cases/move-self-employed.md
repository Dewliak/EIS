# Case M2 — Moving: Self-employed / Freelancer / Business

**Who:** Portuguese citizen moving to work **for themselves** — freelancer, liberal profession, or
running a business. *(Art. 7(1)(a) Directive 2004/38/EC — "self-employed".)*

---

## 1. Information we provide (website content)

- **No visa, no residence permit** — freedom of movement.
- You must **register your business activity** in Germany, not just your address:
  - **Freiberufler** (liberal professions — designers, developers, consultants, writers, teachers,
    artists, health professions) → register with the **Finanzamt** (tax office), get a **Steuernummer**.
  - **Gewerbe** (commercial/trade — shop, agency, café) → register at the **Gewerbeamt**.
- **Health insurance is mandatory for everyone in Germany** (universal obligation). Self-employed
  arrange it themselves — statutory (GKV, voluntary) or private (PKV).
- **Pension/social security** is voluntary for self-employed (except artists/press via KSK), but
  you still pay income tax + trade tax (if Gewerbe).
- **Anmeldung** still required within 14 days of moving in.

## 2. Procedures

1. **Housing** → rental contract → **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**.
2. **Anmeldung** at Bürgeramt (14 days).
3. **Steuer-ID** arrives by post (auto).
4. **Register the business:**
   - Freiberufler → Finanzamt **Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung** (tax registration
     questionnaire) → receive **Steuernummer**.
   - Gewerbe → **Gewerbeanmeldung** at the local Gewerbeamt (trade office) → forwarded to Finanzamt.
5. **Health insurance** — join GKV (voluntary) or PKV.
6. **Pension insurance** — optional (Deutsche Rentenversicherung voluntary; KSK for artists).
7. **Bank account** (business account recommended).

## 3. Deadlines

| Trigger | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Move-in | Anmeldung | **14 days** |
| Start of activity | business registration (Finanzamt/Gewerbe) | before trading |
| After registration | Steuernummer issued | ~2–4 wk |
| VAT (if applicable) | register for VAT (USt) | as needed |
