# Case M3 — Moving: Studies

**Who:** Portuguese citizen enrolled (or enrolling) in a recognized German university or
vocational school. *(Art. 7(1)(c) Directive 2004/38/EC.)*

---

## 1. Information we provide (website content)

- **No visa, no residence permit** — freedom of movement. Unlike non-EU students, you do **not**
  need a blocked account (Sperrkonto) or a study visa.
- You must still prove **enrolment + sufficient resources + health insurance** (the Art. 7(1)(c)
  conditions).
- **University admission** uses your Portuguese school-leaving / degree certificates (recognized
  across the EU under the Professional Qualifications Directive / Lisbon Convention).
- **Student health insurance:** discounted public student rate (approx. €80–130/month under 30),
  or an equivalent — you need proof of insurance **before enrolment**.
- You may work part-time while studying (EU students have no work-hours cap; the 140/280-day rule
  applies to non-EU permit holders).

## 2. Procedures

1. **Admission:** apply to the university with your Portuguese qualifications → get admission.
2. **Housing** → rental contract → **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**.
3. **Health insurance:** join a public insurer on the student rate (or equivalent) **before enrolment**.
4. **Enrol (Immatrikulation):** submit insurance proof + ID → get matriculation confirmation.
5. **Anmeldung** at the Bürgeramt within 14 days of moving in.
6. **Steuer-ID** arrives by post (auto).
7. **Bank account** for rent/expenses (after Anmeldung).

## 3. Deadlines

| Trigger | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Move-in | Anmeldung | **14 days** |
| Before enrolment | health insurance proof | before enrolment |
| Enrolment | Immatrikulation | per university calendar |
| Each semester | re-enrol + pay semester fee | per university |
