# Case M4 — Moving: Job-seeking

**Who:** Portuguese citizen moving to Germany **to look for work** (not yet employed). *(Art.
14(4)(b) Directive 2004/38/EC — a distinct, time-limited category.)*

---

## 1. Information we provide (website content)

- **No visa, no residence permit** — freedom of movement.
- You may stay **more than 3 months while genuinely seeking work**, but this is a **time-limited
  window** — generally up to **6 months**. You must show a **reasonable prospect of finding work**
  and be **seriously pursuing** the search.
- You need **sufficient means of subsistence + health insurance** (you are not yet earning).
- Register with the **Bundesagentur für Arbeit (Agentur für Arbeit)** — job centre, EURES advisor,
  and (if entitled) unemployment-benefit transfer from Portugal.
- Once you find a job → this case becomes **M1 (worker)**.

## 2. Procedures

1. **Housing** → rental contract → **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**.
2. **Anmeldung** at Bürgeramt within 14 days.
3. **Health insurance** — arrange yourself (GKV voluntary or PKV) until employed.
4. **Register at Agentur für Arbeit** (arbeitsagentur.de) — job search, counselling.
5. **Keep evidence of your job search** (applications, interviews) — the foreigners authority may
   ask for proof of serious search + prospects.
6. **On finding a job** → M1: employer takes over insurance + social security.

## 3. Deadlines

| Trigger | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Move-in | Anmeldung | **14 days** |
| Job search | find work (6-month window) | ~6 months |
| On hire | switch to M1 (worker) | employer-driven |
