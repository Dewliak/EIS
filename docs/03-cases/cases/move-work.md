# Case M1 — Moving: Work (employed)

**Who:** Portuguese citizen with a **job contract at a German employer**. *(Art. 7(1)(a)
Directive 2004/38/EC; §2 Freizügigkeitsgesetz/EU.)*

---

## 1. Information we provide (website content)

- You need **no visa and no residence permit** — freedom of movement. Your employment contract is
  your proof of the right to stay >3 months.
- You must register your address (**Anmeldung**) at the local **Bürgeramt** within **14 days** of
  moving in — fine up to **€1,000** if missed.
- Your **employer** handles most of the chain: health insurance, social security (pension) number,
  and income-tax registration (Lohnsteuer).
- Your **Steuer-ID** (tax ID) is assigned **automatically** after Anmeldung and sent by post.

## 2. Procedures

1. **Housing** → signed rental contract → get **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung** (landlord confirmation).
2. **Anmeldung** at the Bürgeramt within 14 days (ID + landlord confirmation + employment contract).
3. **Steuer-ID** arrives by post (~1–2 weeks, automatic).
4. **Health insurance** — employer registers you with a public insurer (choose TK/AOK/Barmer).
5. **Social security number** (Rentenversicherungsnummer) — issued via employer/pension fund.
6. **Bank account** (IBAN) — for salary, after Anmeldung.
7. **§5 Aufenthaltsanzeige** (notice of residence) may be requested by the foreigners authority —
   submit only if asked.

## 3. Deadlines

| Trigger | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Move-in | Anmeldung | **14 days** (fine up to €1,000) |
| After Anmeldung | Steuer-ID | auto (~1–2 wk) |
| Before/as of start | health insurance + social security | employer-driven |
