# 02 — Traveling Case (short stay < 3 months)

Persona: **Tiago** — Portugal (Lisbon) → Germany (Berlin), 3 weeks.

## Legal baseline (verified)
- As an EU citizen entitled to freedom of movement, you need **no visa and no residence permit**
  to enter or stay in Germany. *(Freedom of Movement Act/EU, in force since 1 Jan 2005;
  EU Equal Treatment Office.)*
- Entry and stay **up to 3 months** are subject to **no conditions or requirements** — a valid
  ID (passport or national ID card) is sufficient. *(eu-gleichbehandlungsstelle.de)*
- No registration (Anmeldung) required for a short, temporary stay.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — Before departure
- [ ] Check passport/national ID valid for the whole trip.
- [ ] Get/verify **EHIC** from Portugal (Segurança Social / health provider) — covers necessary
      healthcare in Germany on the same terms as locals for a temporary stay.
- [ ] Optional: private travel insurance (repatriation, luggage, cancellation).
- [ ] Confirm EU mobile roaming ("roam like at home") + data.

### Step 2 — On arrival
- [ ] Nothing to register. No border checks for intra-Schengen travel.
- [ ] Keep ID on person (German authorities may require proof of identity).

### Step 3 — During the stay (3 weeks)
- [ ] Use EHIC if healthcare needed — show it at a doctor/hospital (via public insurer).
- [ ] Work conference: brief attendance does **not** create a German tax/social-security
      obligation. No Anmeldung, no Steuer-ID, no bank account needed.

### Step 4 — Boundary condition (if stay extends / turns into a move)
- [ ] If he actually **moves** his residence to Germany, the **14-day Anmeldung** rule activates
      immediately upon moving in (not after 3 months — Germany differs from Portugal here).
      See `MOVING-CASE.md`.

## Documents needed (traveling)
| Document | Required? | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valid national ID / passport | ✅ | Portugal | Only hard requirement |
| EHIC | Recommended | Portuguese health provider | Temporary state healthcare |
| Private travel insurance | Optional | Any insurer | Extra coverage |

## Information needed (traveling)
- Length of stay (confirm <3 months)
- Purpose (tourism / conference / business)
- Health coverage (EHIC / private)
- Whether the trip will become a relocation (triggers Anmeldung)

## Deadlines (traveling)
| Trigger | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Arrival — no registration | — |
| Day 90 (3 months) | Right to stay becomes conditional | assess |
| If residence moves | Anmeldung | 14 days |
