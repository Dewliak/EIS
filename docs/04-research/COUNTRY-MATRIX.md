# 11 — 27-Country Registration Matrix (Master)

**The seed dataset for the EIS platform.** What an EU/EEA/CH citizen (freedom of movement — no
visa, no residence permit) must do to **move in** (long stay >3 months) to each of the 27 EU
member states.

- Primary sources only; secondary cross-checks flagged.
- `UNVERIFIED` = no authoritative amount/name found in a primary source — **do NOT publish without
  confirmation**.
- Baseline (all 27): ≤3 months = valid ID only, no formalities. EHIC bridges temporary care; for
  residency you register with the destination health system (S1 form if transferring).
- Researched Aug 2026. Re-verify fees/fines before shipping content.

## B. Clock taxonomy — the 4 archetypes the platform must encode

The per-country deadline collapses into **four distinct patterns**:

| Archetype | Countries | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Immediate-on-arrival** (short fixed window) | Austria (3d), Netherlands (5d), Luxembourg (8d), Slovakia (10wd report), **Germany (14d)** | Register within days of moving in/arriving. |
| **2. Within-3-months window** | Spain, Belgium, Slovenia, Romania, Bulgaria, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Finland, Denmark, Malta, Cyprus (4 mo), Sweden (≥1 yr) | Register any time before the 3-month mark. |
| **3. Threshold + N days** (after the 3-month mark) | **Portugal (30d)**, Croatia (8d), Slovakia (register 30d), Hungary (93d), Poland (day-after), Greece (after month 3) | The clock *starts* when the 3-month threshold passes. |
| **4. None / optional** | France, Ireland (none), Czechia (optional), Italy (no fixed clock) | No registration obligation for EU citizens. |

> **The killer detail:** a user in archetype 1 who waits "3 months" (archetype 2's logic) is
> already €1,000 late in Germany. A user in archetype 3 who registers "on arrival" (archetype 1's
> logic) in Portugal is *too early* — the CRUE clock hasn't started. This is the exact per-country
> logic the platform exists to surface.

## C. Cross-cutting insights

1. **No EU country requires a residence permit from EU citizens.** Every "document" is a
   *registration* (Anmeldung, CRUE, CUE, annexe 19, registreringsbevis…). Never present these as
   "visas" or "permits."
2. **Mandatory landlord form is rare.** Only Germany (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung §19 BMG), Austria
   (Meldezettel signature), and Slovakia (notarised accommodation proof) require a specific form.
   Everywhere else, a lease/address proof suffices.
3. **Tax ID acquisition has 3 modes:** auto (DE, NL, BE, LU, SE), assigned-with-registration (ES,
   EE, LV, LT, PL, RO), or apply-separately (the rest). This is a distinct user step to model.
4. **Fines are mostly unpublished.** Only 6 countries have a confirmed fine (DE €1,000, PT
   €400–1,500, AT €726, BE €200, CY €2,500, NL up to €325). For the other 21, either no fine
   exists or — more commonly — the amount isn't published on the primary portal. The platform
   should say "penalties may apply" rather than invent a number.
5. **Some countries have a second registration layer** (Spain's padrón, Estonia's ID card, Croatia's
   optional residence card). The data model must allow **1..N registration steps**, not one.

- **Fine amounts unverified for 21 of 27 countries** — only DE, PT, AT, BE, CY (and NL max) have
  published figures. Treat all others as "not published," not "no fine."

- **Bulgaria** — residence-certificate fee + address-proof specifics.
- **Lithuania** — state fee ~€10 (from university guidance pages, not the official fee schedule).
- **Latvia** — fine amount + certificate fee (the €4.27 is the *address declaration* fee).
- **France** — confirm no formal registration obligation in first 5 years (F2651 implies, not explicit).

  *negative* findings (not published), not confirmed zeros.

  explicit politsei.ee deadline.
