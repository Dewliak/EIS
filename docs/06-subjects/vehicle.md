# Subject — Vehicle (Portugal → Germany)

## Traveling (< 3 months)

- **Drive with your Portuguese licence** — EU/EEA licences are recognized in Germany, no exchange
  needed.
- **Your own car** with Portuguese plates is fine for a temporary stay (visits, holidays).
- Green-card insurance cover + EU-wide validity of your PT motor insurance apply.

### Documents
| Doc | Notes |
|---|---|
| Portuguese driving licence | valid in DE, no exchange |
| PT car registration + insurance | fine for temporary stay |

## Moving (> 3 months)

**Key rule:** once you establish residence, German rules for vehicles kick in after a grace period
(commonly ~**6 months** — verify with the local **Zulassungsstelle** / **Führerscheinstelle**).

### Procedures
1. **Driving licence** — EU licences remain valid; you *may* (not must) exchange for a German one
   for convenience. Non-EU licences must be exchanged after 6 months.
2. **Car re-registration** — re-register your PT-plated car in Germany (Zulassungsstelle):
   - **TÜV/HU** inspection, **eVb** insurance confirmation, **customs** clearance (if applicable),
     proof of ownership, ID, Anmeldung.
3. **Vehicle tax** (Kfz-Steuer) — assessed on registration.
4. **Insurance** — take out German liability insurance (Pflichtversicherung).

### Deadlines
| Trigger | Action | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Establish residence | re-register PT car / exchange licence | ~6 months (verify) |
| On re-registration | TÜV + insurance + tax | at registration |

### Documents
| Doc | Issuer | URL |
|---|---|---|
| Driving licence (PT) | valid as-is; optional exchange | local Führerscheinstelle |
| Car registration (Zulassung) | Zulassungsstelle | city portal (e.g. service.berlin.de) |
| TÜV/HU certificate | TÜV / DEKRA | https://www.tuev.de |
| eVb insurance confirmation | German insurer | — |
| Vehicle tax (Kfz-Steuer) | Zoll / Finanzamt | https://www.zoll.de |
