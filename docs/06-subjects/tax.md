# Subject — Tax (Portugal → Germany)

## Traveling (< 3 months)

- **No German tax obligation** for a short stay — you stay below the **183-day** threshold and
  remain Portuguese tax resident.
- No Steuer-ID, no filing, no registration needed for a temporary visit.

### Info
- 183 days in a 12-month window (or "centre of vital interests" in DE) = tax-residency trigger —
  not reached on a <3-month trip.

## Moving (> 3 months)

**Key rule:** living in Germany **>183 days/year** (or having your main home there) makes you a
**German tax resident** — worldwide income becomes taxable in Germany. The **Germany–Portugal
Double Taxation Agreement (DTA, in force since 1982)** prevents double taxation.

### Procedures
1. **Steuer-ID** — assigned automatically after Anmeldung, sent by post.
2. **Employee:** employer withholds **Lohnsteuer** (payroll tax) automatically.
3. **Self-employed:** register at **Finanzamt** → get a **Steuernummer** (separate from Steuer-ID).
4. **Annual tax return** (Einkommensteuererklärung) — file via **ELSTER**.
5. For dual-residency edge cases, request a **tax-residency certificate** to avoid double taxation.

### Deadlines
| Trigger | Action | Window |
|---|---|---|
| After Anmeldung | Steuer-ID arrives | ~1–2 weeks |
| Start of work | Lohnsteuer (payroll) / Steuernummer (self-empl.) | as of start |
| Year end | annual tax return | by 31 July (following year) |

### Documents
| Doc | Issuer | URL |
|---|---|---|
| Steuer-ID | BZSt (auto) | https://www.bzst.de |
| Steuernummer (self-employed) | Finanzamt | https://www.elster.de |
| Tax-residency certificate | Finanzamt | https://www.elster.de |
