# Subject — Social security (Portugal → Germany)

## Traveling (< 3 months)

- A short tourist/business trip keeps you in the **Portuguese** social-security system — nothing
  to do in Germany.
- **Posted worker** (sent temporarily by your PT employer): you need an **A1 certificate** to
  prove PT coverage continues — applies to business trips and postings up to **24 months** (and
  the **25% multi-state** rule for people working in several countries).

### Documents
| Doc | Issuer | URL |
|---|---|---|
| A1 certificate (if posted/working) | Segurança Social (PT) | https://www.seg-social.pt |

## Moving (> 3 months)

**Key rule:** once you work/reside in Germany, you move into the **German** social-security system.
EU coordination rules (Regulation (EC) 883/2004) decide which country's rules apply.

### Procedures
1. **Rentenversicherungsnummer** (pension number) — issued via employer / Deutsche
   Rentenversicherung, needed for payroll.
2. **S1 form** (healthcare) — transfer health entitlement from PT.
3. **U2 form** (unemployment) — transfer your PT unemployment benefit while you job-seek in DE
   (up to 3–6 months).
4. **A1** if you remain a posted worker rather than a local employee.
5. Contributions (pension, health, unemployment, care, accident) are deducted via payroll.

### Deadlines
| Trigger | Action | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Start work | Rentenversicherungsnummer + registration | as of start |
| Before leaving PT | request S1/U2 from Segurança Social | before departure |
| Job-seek | U2 unemployment transfer | up to 3–6 months |

### Documents
| Doc | Issuer | URL |
|---|---|---|
| Rentenversicherungsnummer | Deutsche Rentenversicherung | https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de |
| S1 (healthcare) | Segurança Social (PT) | https://www.seg-social.pt |
| U2 (unemployment) | IEFP / Segurança Social (PT) | https://www.iefp.pt |
| A1 (posted worker) | Segurança Social (PT) | https://www.seg-social.pt |
