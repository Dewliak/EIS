# Subject — Health (Portugal → Germany)

## Traveling (< 3 months)

- **EHIC** (European Health Insurance Card, free from Portugal) covers **medically necessary**
  state healthcare on the same terms as locals.
- EHIC is **not travel insurance** (no repatriation/cancellation cover) and is **not** valid once
  you become a resident.
- Emergency number: **112**.

### Documents
| Doc | Issuer | URL |
|---|---|---|
| EHIC | Segurança Social / health provider (PT) | https://ehic.europa.eu/ |
| Private travel insurance (optional) | any insurer | — |

## Moving (> 3 months)

**Key rule:** health insurance is **mandatory for everyone in Germany** (universal obligation).
EHIC only bridges temporary stays — as a resident you join a German insurer.

### Procedures
1. **S1 form** (from Portugal, Segurança Social) — transfers your healthcare entitlement to
   Germany. Needed for pensioners, posted workers, and some other cases; employed people are
   usually registered by their employer instead.
2. Join a **public (GKV)** insurer — TK, AOK, Barmer — or **private (PKV)** if eligible
   (self-employed, high earners, students choose student-rate GKV).
3. **S1 / GKV card** → access doctors/hospitals; **Anmeldung** is usually required first.
4. Register with a **Hausarzt (GP)** once insured.

### Deadlines
| Trigger | Action | Window |
|---|---|---|
| On arrival | join GKV/PKV | immediately (mandatory) |
| Before enrolment (student) | student-rate GKV | before enrolment |

### Documents
| Doc | Issuer | URL |
|---|---|---|

| GKV/PKV membership | TK / AOK / Barmer / PKV | https://www.tk.de |
