# Subject — Family (Portugal → Germany)

## Traveling (< 3 months)

- Family members can **visit freely** (freedom of movement) — no registration for a short stay.
- Each person carries a valid ID/passport; non-EU family members carry their passport.

### Documents
| Doc | Notes |
|---|---|
| Valid ID / passport (each member) | only requirement |
| (Non-EU family) valid passport | may need a visa depending on nationality |

## Moving (> 3 months)

**Key rule:** family members who join/accompany an EU citizen have the **same freedom-of-movement
right** — regardless of their nationality (even non-EU). They can **work from day one**.

### Procedures
1. **Proof of relationship** — marriage/birth certificate (translated to German if not in
   PT/EN/DE; possibly apostilled).
2. **Anmeldung** — one adult can register the whole family at the Bürgeramt (bring everyone's ID).
3. **Health insurance** — family members are co-insured under the main citizen's GKV.
4. **Non-EU family members** additionally apply for a **residence card** at the foreigners
   authority (Ausländerbehörde) — this is the only "permit-like" step.

### Deadlines
| Trigger | Action | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Move-in | Anmeldung (whole family) | 14 days |
| On arrival | health insurance (co-insured) | with main citizen |
| Non-EU family | residence card application | as soon as possible |

### Documents
| Doc | Issuer | URL |
|---|---|---|
| Marriage / birth certificate (+ translation/apostille) | Conservatória (PT) | https://www.civilonline.mj.pt |
| Anmeldung → Meldebescheinigung | Bürgeramt | service.berlin.de |
| Health insurance (co-insured) | main citizen's insurer | https://www.tk.de |
| Residence card (non-EU family) | Ausländerbehörde | local foreigners authority |
