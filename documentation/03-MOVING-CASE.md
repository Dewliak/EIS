# 03 — Moving Case (long stay > 3 months)

Persona: **Beatriz** — Portugal (Porto) → Germany (Berlin), permanent job relocation.

## Legal baseline (verified)
- EU citizens need **no visa and no residence permit** — freedom of movement. *(Freedom of
  Movement Act/EU, 1 Jan 2005.)*
- Right to stay **>3 months** if you *(eu-gleichbehandlungsstelle.de)*:
  1. work as employed **or** self-employed, **or**
  2. do vocational training, **or**
  3. look for work with a reasonable prospect of success, **or**
  4. are not gainfully employed but have sufficient means of subsistence + health insurance
     (also students), **or**
  5. have legally resided in Germany ≥5 years (permanent right of residence).
- **Anmeldung (registration):** everyone — Germans included — must register their address at the
  local **Bürgeramt / registration office** within **14 days (2 weeks)** of moving in.
  *(§17 Bundesmeldegesetz, BMG.)* Fine up to **€1,000** if missed.
- **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung (landlord confirmation)** is required — and **the tenancy agreement
  does NOT replace it** *(§19(3) BMG)*.

> ⚠️ Key difference vs Portugal: Germany registers **immediately on move-in (14 days)** — there is
> no "30 days after the 3-month mark" grace period and no CRUE-style certificate. The Anmeldung
> *is* the residence registration.

## Step-by-step (order matters)

### Step 1 — Housing + landlord confirmation
- [ ] Sign **rental contract**.
- [ ] Get **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung** signed by the landlord/agent
      (form `assets/pdf/wohnungsgeberbestaetigung_berlin.pdf`). The lease alone is insufficient.

### Step 2 — Anmeldung (address registration) — within 14 days of moving in
- [ ] Book an appointment at the local **Bürgeramt** (registration office).
- [ ] Bring:
      - valid passport / national ID
      - signed Wohnungsgeberbestätigung
      - (recommended) employment contract — the office forwards this to the foreigners authority
- [ ] In person, or via a representative with written power of attorney. One adult can register
      the whole family (bring everyone's ID).
- [ ] Receive **Meldebescheinigung** (registration certificate) — proof of address.

### Step 3 — Tax ID (Steuer-ID) — automatic
- [ ] The **Steuerliche Identifikationsnummer** is assigned automatically after Anmeldung and
      sent by post (≈1–2 weeks). No separate application.

### Step 4 — Health insurance — mandatory
- [ ] Join a **public health insurer** (gesetzliche Krankenkasse, e.g. TK/AOK/Barmer) or private
      if eligible. Employer typically registers you once you start work.
- [ ] EHIC only bridges temporary stays — for residency you need German cover.

### Step 5 — Social security number (Rentenversicherungsnummer)
- [ ] Issued via employer / pension insurance fund (Deutsche Rentenversicherung) or health insurer
      — arrives by post. Needed for payroll contributions.

### Step 6 — Bank account
- [ ] Open account (IBAN) — requires Anmeldung/Meldebescheinigung + ID. Needed for salary.

### Step 7 — Foreigners authority (only if requested)
- [ ] The Ausländerbehörde normally assumes freedom-of-movement requirements are met. It **may**
      request: employment confirmation, self-employment proof, means + insurance proof, or
      job-search proof. Respond only if asked.

### Step 8 — Family (if applicable)
- [ ] Family members (any nationality, incl. non-EU) who join an EU citizen have the same
      freedom-of-movement right and register the same way (Anmeldung).

## Documents needed (moving) — full checklist
| # | Document | Issuer | Prereq |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Valid ID/passport | Portugal | — |
| 2 | Employment contract / self-employment proof | Employer / self | — |
| 3 | Wohnungsgeberbestätigung | Landlord / agent | signed lease |
| 4 | Anmeldung → Meldebescheinigung | Bürgeramt | ID + landlord confirmation |
| 5 | Steuer-ID (tax ID) | Federal Central Tax Office (BZSt) | auto after Anmeldung |
| 6 | Health insurance membership | Public/private insurer | mandatory |
| 7 | Rentenversicherungsnummer (social security) | Pension fund / insurer | employment |
| 8 | Bank account (IBAN) | Bank | Anmeldung + ID |

## Information needed (moving)
- Date of move-in (starts the 14-day Anmeldung clock)
- Employment status + contract (which >3-month condition applies)
- Landlord details + confirmation (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung)
- Address (which Bürgeramt)
- Health insurance status
- Family members + nationalities (all register via Anmeldung)

## Deadlines (moving)
| Trigger | Action | Deadline | Fine if missed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move-in | Anmeldung at Bürgeramt | **14 days** | up to €1,000 |
| After Anmeldung | Steuer-ID arrives | ~1–2 weeks (auto) | — |
| Start work | Health insurance + social security | before/as of start | legal requirement |
| 5 years residence | Permanent right of residence | — | — |
