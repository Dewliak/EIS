# Case T1 — Traveling: Short stay / visiting (< 3 months)

**Who:** any Portuguese citizen going to Germany for up to 3 months — tourism, business trip,
conference, remote work, visiting family. *(Art. 6(1) Directive 2004/38/EC.)*

---

## 1. Information we provide (website content)

- **You need no visa, no residence permit, no registration** — EU freedom of movement.
- Your stay up to **3 months** has **no conditions or formalities** beyond holding a **valid
  passport or national ID card**.
- Germany requires everyone ≥16 to carry a valid ID document.
- Health: carry your **EHIC** (European Health Insurance Card, issued free in Portugal) for
  necessary state healthcare during the temporary stay.
- Your Portuguese mobile plan works in Germany ("roam like at home"); no roaming fees within the EU.

## 2. Procedures

1. **Before departure:** verify passport/ID validity; get EHIC from Segurança Social (Portugal).
2. **Arrival:** nothing to register. No border checks (Schengen).
3. **During stay:** use EHIC for any healthcare need.
4. **If you stay past 3 months** → this becomes a *moving* case (M1–M6) — see `12-SUBCATEGORIES.md`.

## 3. Deadlines

| Trigger | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival | none | — |
| Day 90 (3 months) | stay becomes conditional → pick M-case | assess then |
