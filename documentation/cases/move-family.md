# Case M6 — Moving: Family member (joining / accompanying an EU citizen)

**Who:** Portuguese citizen joining or accompanying a family member who holds EU freedom of
movement in Germany — spouse / registered partner, child under 21, dependent parent, or another
dependent relative. *(Art. 7(1)(d) + Art. 2(2) Directive 2004/38/EC.)*

---

## 1. Information we provide (website content)

- **No visa, no residence permit** — family members of an EU citizen have the **same freedom of
  movement right as the citizen**, regardless of their own nationality (even non-EU).
- You can **work from day one** — no separate work permit.
- You register the same way as the main citizen (**Anmeldung**, 14 days).
- Non-employed family members must show **sufficient means + health insurance** (typically covered
  by the main citizen's situation).
- This case is for **EU family members**. **Non-EU family members** additionally apply for a
  **residence card** at the foreigners authority (separate sub-case — out of scope for PT→DE EU
  citizens).

## 2. Procedures

1. **Proof of relationship** — marriage certificate, birth certificate (translated if not in
   DE/EN/PT, and possibly apostilled).
2. **Housing** (joint) → rental contract → **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**.
3. **Anmeldung** at Bürgeramt within 14 days — one adult can register the whole family (bring IDs).
4. **Health insurance** — via the main citizen (family members are co-insured under statutory GKV).
5. **Steuer-ID** arrives by post (auto) for each person.
6. **§5 Aufenthaltsanzeige** if requested by the foreigners authority.

## 3. Deadlines

| Trigger | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Move-in | Anmeldung (whole family) | **14 days** |
| On arrival | health insurance (co-insured) | with main citizen |
| If requested | §5 notice of residence | as requested |
