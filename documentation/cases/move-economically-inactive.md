# Case M5 — Moving: Economically inactive (sufficient resources)

**Who:** Portuguese citizen with **no German employer and no German business** — retiree /
pensioner, **remote worker / digital nomad** (working for a PT/foreign employer), or anyone living
off savings/investments. *(Art. 7(1)(b) Directive 2004/38/EC.)*

---

## 1. Information we provide (website content)

- **No visa, no residence permit** — freedom of movement. But you must satisfy **two conditions**:
  1. **Sufficient resources** — enough income/savings that you won't claim German welfare.
  2. **Comprehensive health insurance** — valid in Germany, covering all major risks.
- **Important distinction:** a remote worker employed by a **non-German** employer is **not** a
  "worker" for German residence purposes (that requires a German employer). You fall under this
  "economically inactive" case.
- **Tax residency:** living in Germany >183 days/year generally makes you a German tax resident
  (worldwide income). Plan for this before moving.
- Health insurance: as a non-employed resident you typically join **private (PKV)** or **voluntary
  statutory (GKV)** — PKV often refuses older applicants, so check eligibility early.

## 2. Procedures

1. **Housing** → rental contract → **Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**.
2. **Health insurance** — arrange comprehensive cover (PKV or voluntary GKV) **before/on arrival**.
3. **Anmeldung** at Bürgeramt within 14 days.
4. **Steuer-ID** arrives by post (auto).
5. **Keep proof of resources** (pension statement, employment contract, bank statements) — the
   foreigners authority may request it.
6. **Bank account** (after Anmeldung).
7. **Tax registration** — file with the Finanzamt (tax residency consequences).

## 3. Deadlines

| Trigger | Action | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Move-in | Anmeldung | **14 days** |
| On arrival | comprehensive health insurance | before/at arrival |
| 183 days / yr | tax residency triggers | assess |
| If requested | submit proof of resources | as requested |
